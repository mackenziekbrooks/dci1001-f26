## Week 10 Homework - research log

Time to start creating your project and seeing happens! 

Exploratory data analysis is the process of working with your data to understand what it's all about. Since data gathering and cleanup can be very time-consuming, the goal of this process is to make sure you're spending your time wisely. It can also be helpful to test your hypothesese as well as the fields you're using or the data you've gathered. Sometimes a data visualization will reveal your 2019 dates all turned out as 1919 or that you inconsistently applied some categories and now your pie chart is off. 

Critically, this process will also prepare you for Week 11 open work time. We won't lecture or discuss readings, instead everyone will work on building and troubleshooting their projcets (plus maybe some peer review). To ensure that you use this time wisely and get help from your professors, this process should help you generate the questions that you want to ask. So, there are no right answers here! Merely documentation of your work, process, thoughts, concerns, etc. 

**Specs:**

* Assemble 5-10 entries, or 10%, in your dataset. This means you should 1) decide on the fields in your spreadsheet and fill out 5-10 entries or 2) gather tweets/comments/posts/etc into your text corpus. 

* If necessary to your project, try out some of the web archiving tools + methods from Week 9. They could help you capture websites, text, or social media content. This is the time to experiment with multiple tools and see what makes sense.

* Prepare a very basic visualization that attempts to answer one or two of your research questions. 

* Embed your data (a screencap will work, or upload file to Canvas) and a visualization on your WordPress site. 

* Plan to spend 1-2 hours on this part of the project. This is the time to do some major lifting! 

* If you consult LLMs to determine how to capture or work with your data, note that in your research log. What did you ask the LLM? What were the results? Were they successful? 

* Research log - Answer the following questions:
	* What did you do to further your project this week? What steps did you take to create this data and visualization?
	* What decisions did you have to make about your project? What did you include and what did you leave out and why? 
	* Does your visualization help illustrate and answer your research question(s)? Why or why not? 
	* How will you plan to capture your data at scale? What tools do you want to use? 
	* What did you struggle with? What questions do you have for your professors?
