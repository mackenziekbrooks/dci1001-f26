## Week 9 homework

* **Proposal**
	* Time to make some decisions about what your project is going to look like! Your proposal should introduce us to the topic, sources, and questions of your research project. Remember, you won't have to execute everything you dream up in this project, but your proposal should aim high from a scholarly perspective. Draw from your annotated bibliography and the ideas, frameworks, and theories that you find there. This week in class we learned about what is possible and what is less possible, how can you imagine querying the web + social media to answer your research questions? 
	* Your proposal should include:
		* A general introduction to the topic, sources, contexts, and methods. You're trying to answer the question, "why should I care?"
		* At least **three** research questions. 
		* At least one paragraph of "literature review" where you summarize the sources found in your annotated bibliography, or others that you find later. The sources should directly inform your project.
		* At least one paragraph that describes your proposed data sources and their contexts. This could be a list of the websites or social media accounts you intend to capture. You should also discuss size, scale, scope, time, users, etc. 
		* One paragraph on any anticipated ethical considerations for your project. 
		* One brief paragraph on your intended methods of data capture and analysis. 
* **Specs:**
	* 500-1000 words
	* Include links to sources + data
	* Citations should be in a formal citation style, but you can use the one you're most comfortable with (Chicago, MLA, etc.)
	* Post to your WordPress. Turn in the link on Canvas. You can set to private, but include the password.
