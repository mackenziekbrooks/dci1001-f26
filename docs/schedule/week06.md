# Week 6 - Social Media: Privacy, Identity, and Algorithms

Social media didn't invent the performance of the self, but it did change the stakes, the audience, and the permanence, and built a detailed data profile based on that performance. And all of that feeds an algorithm that decides what you see next. This week we look at the privacy costs and the cultural impact of social media and the way we use it.
