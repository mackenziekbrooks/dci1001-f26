## Week 4 homework

* **Digital Museum**
	* **Goal:** This week we learned that if we want to save something for posterity, we have to make that decision now. Your homework this week is to step into the role of curator of a digital museum. What do you want to save from now or the recent past? 
	* You'll create a new page on your Neocities website for your digital museum. Use your new HTML/CSS skills to give it an appropriate look and feel. 
	* Select five related websites or other pieces of digital content and save them in the Internet Archive. Write a 50-100 word explanation of the site - what it is, why it matters, and whether there is any dynamic content that might not have saved properly. 
	* Include a curatorial statement - 200-300 words on this topic and why it matters (to you). Why save content around this topic? How do these sites fit into a larger context or culture (or not)? What could someone from the future learn about you or your time on the planet with these sites? What was difficult about capturing these sites? Are there ethical or legal considerations with the content?
	* This is your lab homework for the week, but also the conclusion of Unit 1. 
	* **Requirements:**
		* 1 new HTML page called something like `digitalmuseum.html` on your Neocities site. 
		* Custom and intentional CSS that reflects the vibe of your topic
		* 200-300 word curatorial statement about your topic
		* 5 captures of websites or web content represented by a screencapture and a link to the Internet Archive crawl. 
		* 50-100 word captions on each site. 
		* Print 2-3 images from the items in your museum to used in class on Tuesday. Be prepared to give us a tour of your exhibit!
