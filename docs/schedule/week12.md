# Week 12 - Presentations

Time to share out about your projects! 

## Presentation structure

Since you worked on a research project prospectus this term, it's time to pitch your project! Imagine that your classmates and professors are a grand funding team, ready to decide if your project should receive funding to continue its work beyond this initial stage. 

* **Presenter's job:**
	* Plan a 5-10 minute pitch that explains your project. 
	* No need to make slides, use your website as the visuals. Data visualizations, screen caps of comments/websites, other visuals are encouraged. 
	* Address the following: 
		* What is this project about? What are the research questions?
		* Why did you pick this topic?
		* How does your project fill a gap that other projects haven't touched (think about your lit review)
		* What were your initial findings? What did you learn from the data and the methods? 
		* What would you do differently if this were a bigger project with more funding? What are the future directions? 

* **Audience's job:**
	* Each audience member will select a particular role on a slip of paper. The role represents the type of reviewer you are for the grant organization. It will prompt you with particular values/concerns. For example, the Ethicist is most concerned that the human subjects are treated fairly and their privacy respected. The Archivist wants to preserve the human record for posterity. The Designer cares that the presentation of the project is top notch. 
	* After each presentation, you are encouraged to ask a question from your role's perspective. Not every person has to ask a question each time, but be listening for evidence or lack of evidence.
	* You are also allowed to ask questions as yourself! 
	* We'll rotate roles throughout the presentations. 
	* Roles: Ethicist, Archivist, Software Engineer, Designer, Time Traveler from 100 years in the future, Treasurer, Social Media Influencer, Scholar, AI Evangelist, Tech Skeptic

* **Recap**
	* On Thursday, we'll recap and let the grant funders decide who they will fund. Which project will the Archivist pick? The Ethicist?
