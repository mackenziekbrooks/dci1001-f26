## Week 2 homework

* **Website upgrade**
	* **Goal:** Level up your HTML and CSS skills. Refine your website after exploring Geocities and the early web.
	* Time to customize your website to meet your [1990s dreams](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TZt-pOc3moc)! No slick or shiny websites here! Embrace the low-tech and practice your new CSS skills by refining your fansite with color, fonts, spacing, etc. You are encouraged to add as much content as you want. 
	* Avoid AI in constructing your site. Instead, rely on the [W3 Schools](https://www.w3schools.com/). Remember, the goal is get the feel for being a website creator in the 1990s. Imagine your mom kicking you off the shared computer so she can use the phone line. You are allowed to use other people's HTML and CSS from other websites you visit. 
	* **Requirements**:
		* One or more background colors, gradients, or patterns. 
		* 4-5 `div` containers with their own classes or IDs. Style those classes separately.
		* 1-2 elements that use a `border`, `padding`, `margin`, and `border radius`.
		* Custom styled links. 
		* Add 3-5 decorative elements. These can be old-fashioned gifs, buttons, animations, etc. Take inspiration from existing Neocities or Geocities sites. 
		* Create a second page and link to it from your home page. 
		* Add one feature that mimics the social/community elements of Geocities. This could be a webring, guestbook, counter, links to friend's sites, etc. 
	* **Reflection**
		* Write a short (2-5 sentences) reflection on your design goals and what you wish you could bring with you to the social internet of today. 
	* Reflection should be included on your website in an HTML comment (see above)
	* **Submit on Canvas:**
		* The link to your Neocities site
	* **Due:** Monday, September 21 at 11:59pm
